# Support

Workflow Viewer for Bitbucket is a free app maintained by one developer, Søren Schytz Birk. Support is given on a best-effort basis through GitHub.

## Get help or report a bug

Open an issue at [github.com/sorenbcoding/workflow-viewer-for-bitbucket/issues](https://github.com/sorenbcoding/workflow-viewer-for-bitbucket/issues).

Include:
- what you did, what you expected and what happened;
- the app version shown under **About** in the viewer;
- for a workflow that renders wrongly: the activity type and a small `.xaml` snippet that shows the problem.

Remove company names, credentials, selectors and other confidential content from any snippet first. Issues are public.

## Response times

New issues get a first answer within 5 working days. Fixes are released when ready and listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Security problems

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
